Luca Brodo

Formal Methods & Software Engineering
{{ site.author.affiliation }}

I work on making formal verification of software-intensive and cyber-physical systems cheaper to apply and easier to keep correct as systems evolve. My research covers property refinement for temporal-logic specifications, timed refinement for safe post-deployment updates of real-time systems, and verifiable self-adaptation for autonomous systems. I am supervised by Stefan Henkler.
